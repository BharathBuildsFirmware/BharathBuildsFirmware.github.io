About

Bharath Builds Firmware is an initiative focused on Embedded Systems Engineering, helping learners build practical skills through project-based learning, interactive labs, simulations, and industry-oriented engineering practices.

The focus is on understanding how hardware and software work together — from circuits, microcontrollers, and sensors to firmware, RTOS, communication, testing, and real-world embedded applications.

The goal: learn by building — starting from fundamental electronics and embedded concepts, progressing toward production-quality firmware and complete embedded systems using tools, workflows, and engineering practices followed in the industry.
